Membership categories

Three categories, as set out in the society's Basic Bylaw.

For licensed practitioners and medical students

Affiliate Membership

Open to healthcare practitioners and students in medicine or a related field.

  • Classified and licensed by the SCFHS, or a medical student or intern
  • Attend the society's scientific activities and use its member benefits
  • Without General Assembly voting or board candidacy
Apply Now

For classified colorectal surgeons

Regular Membership

The society's core category, carrying full rights in the General Assembly.

  • Classified in colorectal or colorectal oncologic surgery
  • Attend and vote at the General Assembly
  • Inspect the minutes and financial records of the society
Apply Now

By decision of the Board

Honorary Membership

The society's highest distinction, conferred on those who have served it or the Kingdom.

  • Granted to figures of standing who have rendered distinguished service
  • The right to take part in Board discussions
  • Without a vote, and not counted toward the quorum

By nomination of the Board

Conditions and subscriptions in full: the society's Basic Bylaw, Articles 8–12.
