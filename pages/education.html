Looking back

SSCRS Annual Forum 2025 · Jeddah

Held at the Jeddah Hilton on 9–11 April 2025 under the theme "Quality and outcomes in colorectal surgery", the 2025 Forum combined two days of scientific sessions with a live-surgery day transmitted from King Abdulaziz University Hospital, and closed with the society's General Assembly.
