Committees of the Society

Under Chapter 5 of the Basic Bylaw, the Board and the General Assembly may form standing or temporary committees, each with a defined mandate of three to five members.

Standing · formed by the Board

Executive Committee

Delegated day-to-day authority between Board meetings so the society's work continues without delay.

Chair To be announced

Standing · includes independent members

Internal Audit Committee

Reviews internal controls, financial records and compliance, and nominates the external auditor.

Chair To be announced

Standing · includes independent members

Nominations & Remuneration Committee

Sets the criteria for Board and leadership roles, and the remuneration policy approved by the General Assembly.

Chair To be announced

Standing · formed by the Board

Scientific & Education Committee

Programmes the Annual Forum, workshops and accredited courses, and reviews the society's clinical guidance.

Chair To be announced

Standing · formed by the Board

Awareness & Community Committee

Leads public campaigns on early detection and screening, and the society's patient-facing material.

Chair To be announced

Temporary · formed by the General Assembly

Elections Committee

Three or more members from outside the society who run each Board election and announce its result.

Term Per election cycle

  • 3–5 members per committee, by Board or General Assembly decision
  • Independent voices audit, nominations and executive committees seat members from outside the society
  • Every two months ordinary meetings, in person or by video, with five days' notice
  • Term of the Board a committee's term ends with the Board that formed it

Source: the Basic Bylaw, Chapter 5, Articles 43–47.
