Founding Members

The surgeons who came together in 2009 to establish a national home for colorectal surgery in the Kingdom.

In 1430H, a group of consultant colorectal surgeons from the Kingdom's leading hospitals agreed that the specialty needed a society of its own: to set standards, to train the next generation and to speak for colorectal health nationally. Their founding assembly in Riyadh established the SSCRS, and the first elected Board followed.
