About the Society

A national home for colorectal surgical excellence

Founded by decree of the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, SSCRS was established to organize scientific work, strengthen professional fellowship, and elevate public awareness of colorectal health across Saudi Arabia.

We serve as the premier platform connecting specialists, trainees, researchers, and healthcare partners — fostering a community dedicated to the highest standards of patient care.

Vision, Mission & Goals

Vision

The trusted national reference

To be the premier national authority for colorectal surgical excellence in Saudi Arabia — setting the standard for practice, education, and research.

Mission

Empower & protect

Goals

  • Raise community awareness about colorectal disease prevention
  • Support research and clinical innovation in colorectal surgery
  • Provide continuing medical education for members at all levels

Awareness

Know the signs, act early

Colorectal disease is highly treatable when found early. These are the points the society most wants the public to know.

Start here

Screening from age 45

Most colorectal cancers grow from polyps that cause no symptoms at all. Screening finds and removes them before they turn, which is why it is offered from 45 — earlier if a close relative has been affected.

Read the national guidelines
  • 01

    Do not wait out bleeding

    Blood in the stool is common and usually benign — but it is never something to monitor on your own. Have it looked at.

  • 02

    Note lasting changes in habit

    A change in bowel pattern that persists beyond a few weeks is worth reporting, even when nothing else feels wrong.

  • 03

    Family history moves the date

    A parent or sibling with colorectal cancer usually means starting screening earlier. Tell your doctor about it.

  • 04

    Daily habits matter

    Fibre, movement, a healthy weight and avoiding tobacco all lower risk over a lifetime. None of them replace screening.

This section is general health awareness, not medical advice. For anything concerning you personally, please consult a qualified physician.

Join the Society

Membership categories

Three categories, each with its own conditions and rights, as set out in the society's Basic Bylaw. Membership is closed to the qualifying categories below.

For healthcare practitioners and medical students

Affiliate Member

SAR 200 per year

What you get

  • Attend the society's scientific activities
  • Access to all benefits the society provides

Conditions

  • Classified healthcare practitioner licensed by the SCFHS
  • Or a medical student, intern, or in a related medical field
  • Practitioners outside the Kingdom: proof of eligibility from their own country

No General Assembly attendance, voting, access to financial statements, or board candidacy.

Apply Now

Article 8 of the Basic Bylaw

For classified colorectal surgeons

Regular Member

SAR 300 per year

What you get

  • Attend and vote at the General Assembly
  • Regular updates on the society's activities
  • Inspect minutes and financial records at the society's premises
  • Call an extraordinary General Assembly jointly with 25% of eligible members
  • Delegate another member in writing to represent you

Conditions

  • Classified in colorectal surgery or colorectal oncologic surgery
  • Payment of the annual subscription
  • Cooperate with the society in achieving its objectives
  • Do nothing that causes the society harm
  • Abide by the resolutions of the General Assembly
Apply Now

Article 9 of the Basic Bylaw

For individual and corporate supporters

Supporting Member

SAR 100,000+ donation, plus the regular subscription

What you get

  • All the rights of a Regular Member
  • Additional votes in board elections, proportional to total fees and unrestricted donations divided by the regular subscription, for one election cycle
  • Corporate supporters vote through their authorised legal representative

Conditions

  • Payment of the regular membership subscription
  • An unrestricted donation of at least SAR 100,000 from the member's own account during the current board's term
  • Cooperate with the society in achieving its objectives
  • Do nothing that causes the society harm
  • Abide by the resolutions of the General Assembly

A donation made after the cut-off counts toward the following election cycle.

Apply Now

Article 10 of the Basic Bylaw

Subscriptions are paid once a year, or on a monthly schedule at the member's request and subject to board approval, and must be settled before the end of the financial year. A member joining part-way through the year pays only the proportion matching the remainder of that year. Honorary membership is granted by decision of the Board of Directors and is not applied for.

Source: the society's Basic Bylaw, Articles 8–12.
