About the Association

A national home for colorectal surgical excellence

Founded by decree of the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties, SSCRS was established to organize scientific work, strengthen professional fellowship, and elevate public awareness of colorectal health across Saudi Arabia.

We serve as the premier platform connecting specialists, trainees, researchers, and healthcare partners — fostering a community dedicated to the highest standards of patient care.

Our Direction

Vision, Mission & Goals

Vision
◉

The Trusted National Reference

To be the premier national authority for colorectal surgical excellence in Saudi Arabia — setting the standard for practice, education, and research.

Mission
◈

Empower & Protect

To support surgical education, promote public awareness, facilitate research exchange, and foster professional collaboration across the Kingdom.

Goals

Our Commitments

  • Organize and support scientific conferences and workshops
  • Strengthen ties between Saudi specialists and international societies
  • Raise community awareness about colorectal disease prevention
  • Support research and clinical innovation in colorectal surgery
  • Provide continuing medical education for members at all levels
