Leadership

Board Members

The society is guided by distinguished surgeons and healthcare leaders from across Saudi Arabia.

Chairperson of the Board

Prof. Samar Alhomoud

Consultant Colorectal Surgeon · Professor of Surgery

Consultant colorectal surgeon at the Cancer Center of Excellence, King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre, and Professor of Surgery at AlFaisal University. An international expert in colorectal cancer screening and prevention who has helped draft national, regional and WHO policy, with publications including NEJM and Lancet Oncology.

  • Founding member of the society
  • Honorary Fellow, ASCRS (2026)
  • Chair, WHO IARC Ethics Committee
  • King Abdulaziz Medal, First Class

Dr. Anmar Faisal Semilan

Colorectal & General Surgery

King Fahad Armed Forces Hospital

Consultant in colorectal and general surgery at King Fahad Armed Forces Hospital, where he directs the General Surgery residents' training programme.

Dr. Rami Alsairafi

Colorectal & General Surgery

Security Forces Hospital · Riyadh

Consultant in colorectal and general surgery in Riyadh, with a clinical fellowship in robotic and laparoscopic colorectal oncology from Yonsei University, Seoul.

Prof. Ahmad Zubaidi

Professor of Colorectal & General Surgery

King Khalid University Hospital · Riyadh

Professor and consultant colorectal surgeon at King Khalid University Hospital, Riyadh, FRCSC, with more than 75 peer-reviewed publications.
